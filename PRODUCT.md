# KaiOS — Product Context

## Register

**Brand** — This repository ships a marketing landing page alongside the product backend. The landing surface is brand-first: design communicates the product promise before the app exists in users' hands.

## Product

**KaiOS** is a text-first Android launcher that replaces the app grid with a single command line. Users articulate intent before the phone responds — through MCP connectors, local apps, and intentional friction for distracting apps.

**Tagline direction:** Your phone, blank until you mean it.

## Target Users

**Primary:** Overwhelmed professionals and knowledge workers (22–40) who recognize excessive phone use but lack tools to change behavior.

**Secondary:** Digital minimalists who want a phone that serves them rather than exploits attention.

## Purpose

Collect early-access waitlist signups while demonstrating the product interaction model through interactive UI mocks on the web.

## Brand Personality

**Intentional · Terminal · Focused**

- Speaks in commands, not marketing fluff
- High-contrast, monospace, hardware-display aesthetic
- Orange accent reserved for active intent and focus states
- Rejects visual noise, dopamine loops, and warm-neutral SaaS defaults

## Anti-References

- Purple gradient SaaS landing pages
- Three-column identical feature card grids
- Hero metric strips (big number + small label)
- Cream/warm paper backgrounds
- App grid metaphors on the marketing page itself

## Strategic Design Principles

1. **Product UI is the hero** — The phone mock is the visual anchor, not stock photography.
2. **Intentional friction as feature** — The blank slate and command line are the differentiator, not a bug.
3. **Monochrome until intent** — Color appears only when something is active or requires attention.
4. **Terminal-native voice** — Copy reads like a system, not a brochure.

## Accessibility

- WCAG AA contrast minimum on all body text
- Reduced motion alternatives for all animations
- Keyboard-accessible interactive mocks where feasible
